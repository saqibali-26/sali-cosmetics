Why Sali

Luxury starts with the detail.

Sali Cosmetics was founded by beauty expert Sali Khalil after years working with prestige names including Estée Lauder, Chanel, Guerlain and Christian Dior. Her original frustration was simple: too many false lashes compromised on quality.

The brand was created to offer beautiful lashes with attention to wearability, comfort, feel and the look of the finished eye.

Founded in 2014

From a makeup artist's frustration to a lash collection.

Sali Khalil began Sali Cosmetics after years in the beauty industry and a desire for higher-quality lashes. She researched lash materials, application and style effects before developing a collection designed for different looks and occasions.

The collection now spans natural hair, 3D and faux mink styles, with choices designed for natural, cat eye, open eye, doll eye and dramatic looks.
